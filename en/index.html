Meet your teacher

Mr Nodar — an international-level English tutor

I've been teaching English for more than ten years, and in recent years entirely online, at an international level. My students live in many countries around the world — from Tbilisi to Berlin to New York — and I teach each of them one-to-one.

I don't believe in “one programme for everyone”. I prepare the materials for each student myself — interactive texts, exercises and vocabulary matched to their level and goal — and I mark every homework assignment personally. At the end of the month we both know exactly what has changed.

My main rule is simple: in the lesson you should be the one talking, not me. A mistake is ordinary working material, not something to be embarrassed about — that's how the fear of speaking disappears fastest.

Why learn with me

Not “another course” — your own teacher

In the first lesson we find your level and your goal — work, relocation, an exam, travel, your child's school — and every lesson is built around it.

How it works

Four steps to results

1

Message me

Send me a couple of lines on WhatsApp or Messenger — what you want and when you're free. I reply the same day.

2

Free intro chat · 20 minutes

We meet on Google Meet for 20 minutes. We talk, I get a feel for your level, and I explain exactly how the lessons work. It is completely free and commits you to nothing.

3

Regular lessons

Twice a week, 75–90 minutes, at a time that suits you. Every lesson: new vocabulary, grammar in context, speaking and homework.

Progress you can see

Homework is corrected, weak points are tracked, and at the end of the month you get a report — so you can see you're really moving forward.

Frequently asked questions

Where do lessons take place?

Online — on Zoom or Google Meet, from home, on your phone or computer. Materials are sent as a link and open on any device.

I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?

Yes. We start at A1 with the most useful phrases, and you'll say something in English in the very first lesson. Fear of speaking disappears fastest in one-to-one lessons.

How soon will I see results?

With regular study (2 lessons a week + homework) most students move up a level in about 6 months, depending on the level and homework. The first change — speaking more confidently — most students notice within the first month.

I live in another country — is the time difference a problem?

No. I teach online students from many countries around the world — from Europe to the USA; lessons run from morning until midnight. We'll find a time that suits us both.

What do the lessons cost?

I'll tell you the price personally when you message me — it depends on your schedule and lesson length. Send me a message on WhatsApp and I'll explain everything.

What if I can't make a lesson?

Let me know in advance and we'll move it — we agree the details together.

Do you teach children?

Yes — teenagers and school-age children. Parents receive a progress report at the end of every month.

Is the intro chat really free?

Yes. It is a 20-minute conversation on Google Meet — we get to know each other, I get a feel for your level, and I explain how the lessons work. You pay nothing and commit to nothing.

Shall we start?

Send me a couple of lines — what you want to learn and when you're free. I'll reply the same day and we'll book your free intro chat.
